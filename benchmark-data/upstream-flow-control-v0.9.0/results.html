Flow control benchmark: Endpoint Picker v0.9.0

Can one shared model pool protect priority traffic, adapt to workload shape, and scale without losing control?

Flow control protected realtime traffic across four production-shaped scenarios.

When demand surged, lower-priority and overloaded workloads absorbed most of the wait.

Priority protection Realtime median p95 time to first token (TTFT) stayed between 404 and 570 ms across all four production scenarios. Flow control engaged in every retained run
Per-GPU throughput held Served requests per second (RPS) per GPU varied by at most 0.6% across one, two, and four model replicas. Nine matched scaling runs across three model-pool sizes
